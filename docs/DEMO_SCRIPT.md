# Demo script: the golden path (about 3 minutes)

Before you start, click **Reset demo** in the top banner. Turn **Review flags** off for a clean presentation, or on when the panel is academic.

| # | Show | Say (one line) | Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home: appeal bar, "Every visit helps restore Hillview" | "One platform ties visiting, learning and giving into a single loop." | D1 |
| 2 | My Hillview → **Continue as Sarah Mitchell** | "Sarah is a Bowral local. One account for everything." | US-01 |
| 3 | What's on → **Register** a free grounds visit → Pay (free) | "Grounds stay free. Registering starts the relationship and manages capacity." | US-04/05 |
| 4 | Next week → book a **house tour** + **craft taster**, add $10 gift, tick 2 consents → Pay | "One basket, one payment. Consent is granular and unticked. GST shown on the invoice, gifts GST-free." | US-06, US-02 |
| 5 | Confirmation: "You'd have saved $X" → See membership → join **Household Friend** | "The nudge converts paid visitors at the exact leak our dashboard identifies." | US-24 |
| 6 | Adopt-a-Stone → **Return verandah posts** → dedication → Adopt | "Every gift is tied to a real part of the building, and learners on the carpentry course work on the same posts." | US-27, US-17 |
| 7 | Estate Console → **Restoration** → Publish update | "Staff update the work once, and every adopter is notified automatically." | US-28 |
| 8 | Header bell → My Hillview: notification + progress bar + timeline | "Sarah sees her post is being restored. That impact update closes the loop." | US-28, US-01 |
| 9 | Console → **Overview**: loop funnel, biggest leak, revenue vs plan | "Paid → Friend is the leak at 5%; venue hire carries revenue; everything's flagged for Finance." | US-34, US-48 |
| 10 | Console → **Supporters** → open Sarah → switch role to **Front-of-house** | "Same record, but front-of-house can't see gift amounts. Privacy by design." | US-32, US-49 |
| 11 | Console → **Demand forecast** | "Python model with real school terms, holidays, Tulip Time and BOM climate; it halves the error of a naive baseline and drives the roster." | US-46/47 |
| 12 | Impact page | "The public sees the same numbers. That's our transparency for media and donors." | US-42 |

**Critique line for the academic panel:** "The history is simulated, so the forecast recovers effects we assumed. The value is the pipeline: once real check-ins exist, it re-estimates from actual data. Every commercial figure is in the assumptions register with an owner and a confidence rating."
